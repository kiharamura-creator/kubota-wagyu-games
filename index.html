FAQ

Who makes these Roblox games?
Kubota Chikusan (株式会社窪田畜産), a family Wagyu cattle farm in Kirishima, Kagoshima, Japan. The games are inspired by real farm life, Japanese Wagyu beef and Japanese food like yakiniku and hamburger steak.
Are the games free?
Yes. All games are free to play on Roblox (PC, Mac, phone and tablet). Some games have optional in-game purchases.
Which languages are supported?
English and Japanese, with automatic translation for Korean, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese and Indonesian.
Which game is good for playing with friends?
Bus Crew! (up to 8 players share one bus), Runner & Builder (2-player co-op), Find the Runaway Wagyu!, Yakiniku Boss Battle! and Rock Paper Tag! are made for playing together.
Is there a realistic cattle farming game?
Yes - Wagyu Farm Life is a realistic Japanese Wagyu farming simulator made by a real Wagyu farm.
Made by Kubota Chikusan (株式会社窪田畜産), a Wagyu cattle farm in Kirishima, Kagoshima, Japan. · kubota-chikusan.com
