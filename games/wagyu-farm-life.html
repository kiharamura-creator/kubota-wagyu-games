Wagyu Farm Life

和牛ファームライフ

A realistic Japanese Wagyu cattle farming simulator made by a real Wagyu farm in Kagoshima, Japan. Cut grass, feed and care for your cows, breed calves, fatten and sell cattle, hire helpers, use tractors and build barns. Neglect your cows and they can get sick.

鹿児島の和牛農家がつくった、リアルな和牛牧場シミュレーション。草刈り・エサやり・繁殖・肥育・出荷、アルバイトや機械、牛舎づくりまで。

Genre: Simulation, Farming
